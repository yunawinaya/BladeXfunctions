# MIN-01 brief: shared stock protection for Item Assembly reservations

From bladexfunctions-fb (the Small Cycle IA session), 22 October 2026, for the session taking MIN-01.

## The task

The BA's MIN-01 (`BA_MINIMUM_FIXES_2026-10-22.md`, section 4 "MIN-01"; RF-01 + RF-03 in `BA_HANDOFF_2026-10-22.md`):

1. **RF-01, lost updates.** IA reservations move stock Unrestricted / Blocked → Reserved through the locked engine
   (INVENTORY_BULK_APPLY, balance and COST locks). The classic stock writers update the same item_balance / item_batch_balance rows
   without those locks, so a reservation racing a classic writer on the same balance row can lose an update (either side). The same gap
   already exists between any two classic writers.
2. **RF-03, owner checks.** A writer that consumes Reserved stock must consume only what is reserved for its own document. Today no
   writer checks who owns Reserved stock.

The BA's acceptance case is A2: competing IAs and each overlapping writer preserve totals and ownership; no lost update; no other
consumer takes an IA's holds.
