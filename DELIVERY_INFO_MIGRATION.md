# Delivery Info — `di_*` Field Migration Map

Mapping from the existing per-delivery-method columns in six modules onto the
new consolidated `di_*` Delivery Info block.

Generated by reading the form JSONs directly (`SQTfullJSON.json`,
`SOfullJSON.json`, `GDfullJSON.json`, `PPfullJSON.json`, `PickingFullJSON.json`,
`PRTfullJSON.json`) — not from field names alone. Component types and stored
value shapes were taken from each field's `el` and `options.props.value`.

---

## Why this exists

Today each module carries five parallel delivery grids — Self Pickup, Courier
Service, Company Truck, Shipping Service, 3rd Party Transporter — and every
module names the same concept differently. `Driver Name` alone lives under six
different column names across the six forms. Because the grids are mutually
exclusive (exactly one is visible for a given `delivery_method`), the five sets
collapse into one shared set of columns.

The divergence is already costing real work: `Picking/MOBILE_DELIVERY_FIELDS_HANDOFF.md`
exists solely to document a 31-field GD→Picking remap table that would be
unnecessary if the names matched.

---

### Standalone — not part of the migration

- **`di_shipping_method`** — individual field, populated on its own.
- **`delivery_method`** (currently `sqt_delivery_method_id`, dict source
  `1897577528487428098`, stores `dict_key`) — individual field. It stays
  because it is the discriminator every `CASE` below keys on.

### Two deliberately shared columns

Both are collision-free — no single delivery method writes to both sources.

- **`di_est_delivery_date`** holds Company Truck's *Est. Delivery Date* **and**
  Courier/Shipping Service's *Shipping Date*. Their arrival dates remain
  separate in `di_est_arrival_date`.
- **`di_freight_charges`** holds Courier/Shipping Service's *Freight Charges*
  **and** Company Truck's *Delivery Cost*.

Consequence: the labels are method-dependent. A courier record displays
"Est. Delivery Date" over what is really its shipping date, and a company-truck
record displays "Freight Charges" over its delivery cost. Either drive the
labels off the selected method or use neutral wording.

## Value-shape conversions

Two rows cannot be copied by value — the destination stores a different shape
than most sources. Migrating these by name writes garbage silently.

### Row 1 — `di_driver_name` wants a Driver **id**

Destination is a `su-fm-table-select` with `props.value: "id"` against Driver
`1983820088528023553`.

- **Already ids:** SQT `ct_driver_name`, SO `ct_driver_name` (Company Truck only).
- **Need a name→id lookup:** all 16 other sources store a typed name string —
  every Self Pickup and 3rd Party column, plus GD/PP/PICK/PRT Company Truck.
- **Unresolvable rows:** a typed name with no matching Driver record has no
  landing spot. Decide up front whether to create Driver records, leave null,
  or keep a text fallback column.

This design also means a customer's own collector must exist in the Driver
master before it can be recorded.

### Row 4 — `di_vehicle_number` wants a Vehicle **id**

Destination is `fm-select` with `props.value: "id"` against Vehicle
`1983800272178065410`. This mirrors row 1 exactly — both destinations are
id-backed pickers whose only already-correct sources are Company Truck.

- **Already ids:** SQT `ct_vehicle_number`, SO `ct_vehicle_number`,
  GD `vehicle_no`, PICK `vehicle_no` — Company Truck only.
- **Need a plate→id lookup:** the other 14 sources store a free-typed plate
  string — every Self Pickup and 3rd Party column, plus PP `vehicle_no` and
  PRT `vehicle_no2` (both Company Truck, both plain inputs).
- **Unresolvable rows:** a typed plate with no matching Vehicle record has no
  landing spot, same as row 1's unmatched driver names.

## Per-module gaps and quirks

### PRT is the ragged one

- No source for `di_validity_of_collection` or `di_tracking_number` → null.
- No TPT driver name → null.
- Its Shipping Method sits under **Courier Service**, not Shipping Service.
- `estimated_ariival` is misspelled in the source form.
- Company Truck's "Estimated Arrival" (`estimated_arrival2`) is mapped to
  `di_est_delivery_date`, not `di_est_arrival_date` — it is a delivery date
  despite the label.
- `freight_charge` is a plain `fm-input`, not a currency component, so values
  may not be 2-dp clean. Round before writing into `di_freight_charges`.

### SO

- `ss_shippping_date` — triple `p`, misspelled in the source form.

### GD / PP

- Self Pickup and Company Truck **share** `driver_name`, `ic_no`, and
  `driver_contact_no`. Harmless for migration (only one method is ever
  populated per record) but it is why the Picking remap table exists.

## Pre-flight checklist

- [ ] Seed any missing Driver records, or decide the null/fallback policy for
      unmatched driver names (row 1).
- [ ] Build the plate→id lookup for the 14 free-text vehicle sources, and
      decide the null/fallback policy for plates with no Vehicle record (row 4).
- [ ] Confirm `delivery_method` is populated on every historical record — it is
      the `CASE` key for all nine multi-source rows. Records with a null method
      cannot be migrated.
- [ ] Round PRT `freight_charge` to 2 dp.
- [ ] Decide the fate of the dropped `shipping_method` columns.
- [ ] Resolve the method-dependent labels on `di_est_delivery_date` and
      `di_freight_charges`.
- [ ] Update the show/hide logic. The five method literals — `"Self Pickup"`,
      `"Courier Service"`, `"Company Truck"`, `"Shipping Service"`,
      `"3rd Party Transporter"` — are compared by name in roughly 30 places,
      including `visibilityMap` in `Quotation/SQTonMounted.js`,
      `func_reset_delivery_method`, and the equivalents in the other five
      modules.
- [ ] Remove the five old grids (`qt_self_pickup`, `qt_courier_service`,
      `qt_company_truck`, `qt_shipping_service`, `third_party_transporter`)
      only after the data is verified in the `di_*` columns.
- [ ] Add every new decimal column to the save workflow's `formatNumber`
      allow-list — `di_freight_charges` is a decimal and a missing entry makes
      the save crash on `multipleOf` validation.
